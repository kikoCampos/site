The method

Three forces. One discipline.

I

Awareness · see

See the system as it is — and measure the gap between the declared value and the lived one.

II

Courage · decide

Give authorship, face the conversations that unlock change, and use diversity as legitimacy.

Consistency · repeat

Measure to keep it alive and accelerate with discipline, until behavior becomes culture.

Change is not an event. It is discipline.

Change doesn't wait for the perfect moment. It starts small, in the right place — and doesn't stop.
