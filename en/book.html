About the book

We live in an era of permanent change.

…yet most organizations still try to transform the future with models from the past. Strategies fail, cultures wear down, and leaders find themselves trapped in answers that no longer work. I propose a new way to lead deep, sustainable transformations, built on three forces: awareness, courage and consistency. It's not about fashionable methods — it's a practical, human guide. The book is published in Portuguese.
