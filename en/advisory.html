The boundary

What it is — and what it isn't.

Advisory is a relationship, not a fixed scope. A clear boundary saves both sides from frustration.

Advisory is

  • Ongoing, confidential counsel for decision-makers.
  • A light, predictable cadence (recurring sessions).
  • Access to think together between sessions.
  • An outside view, with repertoire and method.
  • A trusted mid/long-term relationship.

Advisory isn't

  • A consulting project with deliverables and a squad.
  • Day-to-day operational execution for the team.
  • A one-off diagnosis (that's a Program).
  • Outsourcing the decision — authorship is always yours.
  • 24/7 availability — it's focus, not an on-call desk.
